Pushing Dexterity and Safety for In-Hand Object Pose Reaching That Works Remarkably Well

We present a sim-to-real recipe for training in-hand object pose reaching policies on a multi-fingered hand at arbitrary wrist orientations and transferring them zero-shot to the real hand. A learned residual safety filter is added to tune performance-safety trade-offs and substantially reduce object drops, with extensive real-robot evaluation.

Vision-Language-Action or World-Action? Just Action Models

We propose Just Action Model (JAM), which separates backbone initialization, future-observation objectives, and inference modules, using an action loss plus a foresight loss that predicts frozen future representations without visual generation. JAM matches or outperforms FastWAM/ImageWAM on LIBERO/LIBERO-Plus/RoboTwin and reduces estimated online training FLOPs by 73% for FastWAM with cached targets.

VLA-HWBench: Where the Time Goes When VLA Models Run on Edge Hardware

VLA-HWBench measures 18 open VLA models on NVIDIA Jetson AGX Thor and AMD Ryzen AI Max+ PRO 395 with a seven-stage call breakdown, analyzing latency effects of compilation, specialized backends, and low-precision optimization. It finds that edge VLA latency is driven more by action-generation method, runtime, and platform than by model size.
